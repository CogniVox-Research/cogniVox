The 2024 annual report highlights strong growth in the technology division, specifically in ERP services. Revenue from cloud computing increased by 45% due to strategic partnerships and efficient infrastructure scaling. However, the retail sector, a secondary market for the company, saw a modest decline of 5% in year-over-year sales. The board is prioritizing the expansion of data centers in Asia next quarter to support the cloud growth.Future plans also include a major investment in AI research and development to maintain a competitive edge.
