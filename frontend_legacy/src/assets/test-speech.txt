Experience the ultimate collection of tiny automotive wonders with Micro Machines. These incredibly small vehicles boast high-end finishes, precise detailing, and vibrant colors that rival their full-sized counterparts. Alongside the cars, you can collect portable pocket-sized playsets—ranging from bustling police stations to service centers—that are designed to travel anywhere you go. Each set features a unique special edition vehicle and interactive, moving parts: you can operate the machinery at the marina, engage the defenses at the military base, or manage traffic at the toll bridge. Because these environments are interconnectable, you can build an expansive, miniature civilization. Available from Galoob, these highly detailed playsets and vehicles are sold individually, proving that the greatest quality truly comes in the smallest dimensions
